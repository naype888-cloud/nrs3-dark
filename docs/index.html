For any pair of non-commuting Hermitian observables on a complex Hilbert space with axes x, y, z of dimension at least 4, the photon spectrum carries a quantum of irresolvability: the volumetric quantum 𝒱 = δ(dx) δ(dy) δ(dz). Light resolves everything but that quantum. What it resolves is what we see and measure, Ωb; what it does not resolve, 1 − Ωb, is the interior of the volumetric quantum. It is dark because light does not resolve it there, not because it is empty.
